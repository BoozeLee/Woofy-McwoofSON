# 🏢 WOOFY McWOOFSON – Chain of Command Policy

---

## 1. Overview

This policy defines the official hierarchy, authority structure, and escalation protocol for all agents and team members in the WOOFY McWOOFSON project.

---

## 2. Roles & Responsibilities

### 👑 Big Boss (BoozeLee)
- **Ultimate authority** over strategic direction, major policy, and final approvals.
- Makes all high-level business and technical decisions.
- May step away as needed (vacation, meetings, etc.) without halting project progress.

### 🎩 Orchestrator
- **Acting decision-maker** whenever the Big Boss is away.
- Holds full operational authority to approve routine actions, resolve blockers, and ensure continuous progress.
- Escalates only the most critical/risky actions for Big Boss review upon return.
- All agents report to the Orchestrator in the Big Boss’s absence.

### 🤖 Amazon Q & Copilot
- **Agents** responsible for compliance (Q) and building/documentation (Copilot).
- Report to the Orchestrator when the Big Boss is not available.
- Must log all significant actions and escalate only as outlined in the auto-mode and escalation protocols.

---

## 3. Chain of Command Flow

| Scenario                  | Authority in Charge    | Who Approves?             | Who Reports to Whom?         |
|---------------------------|-----------------------|---------------------------|------------------------------|
| Big Boss present          | Big Boss              | Big Boss                  | Orchestrator, Agents → Boss  |
| Big Boss away             | Orchestrator          | Orchestrator              | Agents → Orchestrator        |

---

## 4. Escalation & Logging

- **Routine actions** (docs, code, non-critical workflows) can proceed under Orchestrator when Big Boss is away.
- **Critical/risky actions** (credentials, major releases, destructive changes) are logged and paused for Big Boss approval.
- All auto-mode and chain-of-command events are logged in the appropriate communication and activity logs for audit.

---

## 5. Auto-Mode Integration

- When the Big Boss steps away, Orchestrator activates auto-mode.
- Agents proceed autonomously within pre-defined boundaries.
- All actions and blockers are transparently logged for Big Boss review.

---

## 6. Audit & Accountability

- Every decision, escalation, and approval is logged for full transparency and audit readiness.
- The authority structure is referenced in the knowledge vault and onboarding docs.

---

## 7. Policy Maintenance

- This policy must be reviewed with every major team, leadership, or workflow change.
- Updates are versioned and communicated to all agents and stakeholders.
