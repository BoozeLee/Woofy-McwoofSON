# 🐾 WoofyMcwoofson Community Code of Conduct

Welcome to the WoofyMcwoofson pack! As contributors, users, and maintainers, we agree to:

- Treat all participants with respect, kindness, and consideration—no ruff behavior!
- Celebrate diversity and welcome pups from all backgrounds and breeds.
- Keep discussions pawsitive and focused on building a fun, secure, and helpful project.
- Bark if you see a problem—report issues promptly and constructively.
- Help new contributors fetch their bearings and feel at home in the doghouse.

**Unleashed conduct will not be tolerated. Violations are grounds for removal from the pack.**  
See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for more details.

Let's keep our community a safe, fun, and collaborative park for all! 🐶🦴
