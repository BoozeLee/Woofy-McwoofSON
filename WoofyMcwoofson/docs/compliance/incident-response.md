# Incident Response Procedures for Security Breaches

## Purpose
This document outlines the procedures to be followed in the event of a security breach affecting the WoofyMcwoofson project. It aims to ensure a swift and effective response to minimize damage and recover from incidents.

## Scope
This incident response plan applies to all team members involved in the development, maintenance, and operation of the WoofyMcwoofson project.

## Incident Identification
1. **Monitoring**: Continuous monitoring of systems and logs to detect anomalies.
2. **Reporting**: Team members must report any suspected security incidents immediately to the Incident Response Lead.

## Incident Classification
Incidents will be classified based on severity:
- **Critical**: Immediate threat to data integrity or availability.
- **High**: Significant risk but not an immediate threat.
- **Medium**: Potential risk that requires investigation.
- **Low**: Minor issues that do not pose a significant risk.

## Response Procedures
1. **Containment**: 
   - Isolate affected systems to prevent further damage.
   - Disable compromised accounts or services.

2. **Eradication**:
   - Identify the root cause of the incident.
   - Remove malicious code or unauthorized access.

3. **Recovery**:
   - Restore affected systems from clean backups.
   - Monitor systems for any signs of weaknesses or further incidents.

4. **Communication**:
   - Notify stakeholders and affected parties as necessary.
   - Provide updates on the status of the incident response.

## Post-Incident Review
1. **Documentation**: Record all actions taken during the incident response.
2. **Analysis**: Conduct a thorough analysis of the incident to identify lessons learned.
3. **Improvements**: Update incident response procedures and security measures based on findings.

## Training and Awareness
Regular training sessions will be conducted to ensure all team members are familiar with incident response procedures and their roles in the event of a security breach.

## Review and Updates
This incident response plan will be reviewed annually and updated as necessary to reflect changes in the project or security landscape.
