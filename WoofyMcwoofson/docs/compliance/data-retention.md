# Data Retention Policy for WoofyMcwoofson Project

## Purpose
This document outlines the data retention policies for the WoofyMcwoofson project, ensuring compliance with legal, regulatory, and organizational requirements.

## Data Retention Guidelines

1. **Personal Data**: 
   - Personal data will be retained only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including for the purposes of satisfying any legal, accounting, or reporting requirements.
   - Personal data will be reviewed annually to determine if it is still necessary for the purposes for which it was collected.

2. **Logs and Audit Trails**:
   - System logs and audit trails will be retained for a minimum of 12 months to support security investigations and compliance audits.
   - After the retention period, logs will be securely deleted or anonymized to protect user privacy.

3. **Backup Data**:
   - Backup data will be retained for a period of 30 days. After this period, backups will be overwritten or securely deleted.
   - Critical backups may be retained for longer periods based on business needs and compliance requirements.

4. **Data Deletion Procedures**:
   - Data deletion will be performed in a manner that ensures complete removal from all storage locations.
   - A record of data deletion activities will be maintained for auditing purposes.

5. **Compliance with Regulations**:
   - The data retention policy will be reviewed and updated regularly to ensure compliance with applicable laws and regulations, including GDPR, HIPAA, and others as relevant.

## Review and Updates
This policy will be reviewed annually and updated as necessary to reflect changes in legal requirements or organizational practices.
