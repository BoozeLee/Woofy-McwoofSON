# 🛡️ Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

To report a security issue or vulnerability, contact the maintainers at [your-email@example.com].  
We will coordinate remediation and disclosure.

## Responsible Disclosure

- Do **not** create public issues for vulnerabilities.
- Email us directly and we will respond within 48 hours.
- We appreciate responsible researchers and will credit disclosures if desired.

## Credential & Secret Policy

- No hardcoded secrets or credentials are permitted in code, docs, or history.
- See [knowledge-vault/CREDENTIAL_ROTATION_AND_HISTORY_CLEANUP.md](knowledge-vault/CREDENTIAL_ROTATION_AND_HISTORY_CLEANUP.md) for rotation and audit procedures.
- All secrets must use environment variables and GitHub encrypted secrets.

## IP Theft & Compliance

- Any attempted intellectual property theft, misappropriation, or unauthorized use of project code, docs, or branding will be prosecuted to the fullest extent of the law.
- All contributors and users must comply with the LICENSE and this policy.

## General Security Best Practices

- Follow principle of least privilege and 2FA on all accounts.
- Escalate any suspected breach or exposure immediately to the project admin.
