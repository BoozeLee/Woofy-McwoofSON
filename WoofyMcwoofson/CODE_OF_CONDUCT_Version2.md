# 🐶 Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open, inclusive, and professional community, we pledge to make participation in WOOFY McWOOFSON a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

- Be kind, respectful, and inclusive.
- No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Respect intellectual property and do not plagiarize or steal code/content.
- No sharing of confidential or sensitive information.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct.

Severe or repeated violations (including IP theft or security breaches) may result in a permanent ban and reporting to legal authorities.
